## The Influencer Who Ate the Leaderboard
Obesidy is the name of a glitchy vending-machine mascot that eats scoreboard numbers. When a fictional celebrity DJ promises a million points for filming a dance at the court, Verity spots the scam and gives the mascot a scoreboard stuck on infinity. The machine gets the hiccups and spits every score back out.

Keep each short between 90 and 120 seconds. Use wide shots, fast physical gags, and a clear visual payoff. Use original characters for the meme roles, including the toilet villain and celebrity DJ.
